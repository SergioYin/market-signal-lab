Market Signal Lab

Research-only static demo: inspect checked-in sample artifacts generated from synthetic local CSV data. This page uses no JavaScript and no remote assets.

Scope boundary: Market Signal Lab does not connect to brokers, read accounts, place orders, fetch current market data, or provide investment advice, recommendations, forecasts, or action guidance.
